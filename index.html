Trends and forecast

Goals and financial independence

Milestones and year in review

Import and export

Private by design

NetWorth has no account and no server holding your data. There's nothing to sign up for and nothing to leak.

Stays on your phone

Your balances live in an encrypted database on your device, with the key in its secure keychain.

No bank logins

You type in your balances. NetWorth never connects to your bank.

App lock and privacy mode

Lock the app with Face ID, fingerprint or passcode, and hide the amounts with one tap.

Your own backup

Optional weekly encrypted backup to your own iCloud Drive or Google Drive.
